Terms and Conditions

By installing or using an Acoustic beta build, you agree to these terms.

Using the beta

The Windows and Android betas are free and participation is voluntary. Acoustic gives you permission to use the beta builds we provide for personal testing. Use the app lawfully and get our permission before redistributing a beta build. Bundled material keeps its own license.

Changes and availability

Beta builds may contain bugs, change, or stop working. We may update or end the beta. You can stop testing at any time and uninstall the app.

Limits and your rights

Acoustic is a sound-masking tool. It does not provide hearing protection, medical treatment, or noise cancellation. To the extent the law allows, beta builds are provided as is and as available, without warranties. Your rights under applicable law still apply.

Privacy Policy

We use only your name and email address for beta testing records. Windows signup saves your name and email address in our beta list, hosted by Supabase. We use those details to manage participation, provide builds, and communicate about the beta. We keep them only as long as needed for those purposes and do not sell them or use them for advertising.

When you sign up for the managed Windows beta, the website displays an activation code. We store a hash of the code and your access end date with your beta record. Acoustic stores its activation credential on your computer and uses it to check access.

What stays on your device

The Acoustic app works offline. Your settings, ratings, sound choices, and session data stay on your device. If you enable microphone access for a session, the app processes that input on your device. The app does not send audio, analytics, or usage data to us.

Website and email

The website saves your theme preference in your browser. Acoustic does not run website trackers or analytics. Our hosting providers may process connection information such as your IP address to serve the site. See the Cloudflare and Netlify privacy policies. Supabase also serves the Windows download and processes the connection information needed for the signup and download. If you email us, Gmail processes your message to deliver and store it. For a beta request, your name and the email address you send from are enough. Please leave out extra personal information and attachments.
